Aim. Fire. Breach the keep.

Turn-based artillery castle siege. Pick three champions, read the wind, pull back and let it fly — crack the enemy walls, open a lane, and blow up their core before they blow up yours.

▶ Play in browser
Free · no install · desktop browser recommended (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge); on a phone, play in landscape. ~45 MB first load, then cached.

How a match plays

1 · Draft your three

Eight champions, three roles. Ranged shinobi arc shots through the wind, samurai raid the enemy castle in person, ninjas slip inside and set it on fire.

2 · Two actions, any split

Every turn: two actions across your team. Lob a shot, send a raid one step, commit arson, plant a trap, patch a wall — each costs one. The castle is a 3×3 building of rooms; champions inside intact rooms are HIDDEN, and walls eat the hit.

3 · Break in — or burn it

Crack a wall and the room behind is exposed; a breach opens a lane that makes core hits land harder. Samurai cross under fire and STRIKE in melee. Ninja fires spread, climb, and leave burnt scars that stop hiding anyone. Repairs: three a match.

4 · Overdrive ends it

Round cap 12; from round 8 the garrison fires back every turn and nothing gets repaired. Matches end — by core kill, by wipe, or by the clock. No stalling.
